# FYP Viva Voce Rubrics

This document contains the complete content of the **Viva Voce Evaluation Rubrics** for the Final Year Project (FYP).

## Summary of Total Marks
- **Section A: Viva Q & A - Knowledge Depth:** 35 Marks (Weightage total: 6*)
- **Section B: Viva Oral Presentation:** 50 Marks (Weightage total: 10)
- **Total Marks:** **85 Marks** (Total Weightage: 16)
  
*\*Note: There is a discrepancy in Section A3 where the marks are allocated as 10 Marks, but the weightage in the rubric is listed as 1. A weightage of 2 is mathematically required to reach 10 Marks on a 0-5 scale.*

---

## A. VIVA: Q & A - KNOWLEDGE DEPTH (35 Marks)
**CO/PO Mapping:** [CO4, PO10]

| Question No | Rubric / Criteria | 0 (Not Available) | 1 (Very Poor) | 2 (Poor) | 3 (Average) | 4 (Good) | 5 (Excellent) | Weightage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A1** | **Response to question with technical and appropriate knowledge (15 Marks)** | No presentation | Demonstrated incomplete knowledge of the topic and unable to respond to questions and feedback. | Demonstrated superficial knowledge of the topic by responding unclearly and inappropriately to questions and feedback. | Demonstrated some knowledge of the topic by responding fairly and appropriately to questions and feedback. | Demonstrated adequate knowledge of the topic by responding clearly and appropriately to questions and feedback. | Demonstrated extensive knowledge of the topic by responding confidently, precisely and appropriately to questions and feedback. | 3 |
| **A2** | **Problem Solving Ability (10 Marks)** | No presentation | Weak mapping of theory concepts with practical problem solving approaches. | Improper mapping of theory concepts with practical problem solving approaches | Moderate mapping of theory concepts with practical problem solving approaches | Efficient mapping of theory concepts with practical problem solving approaches | Successfully mapping of theory concepts with practical problem solving approaches. | 2 |
| **A3** | **Quality of answer and answer (10 Marks)** | Have one or more following items during question and answer:<br>1) Do not understands all the questions<br>2) Unable to answer all of the questions | Have one or more following items during question and answer:<br>1) Do not understands most of the questions<br>2) Unable to answer most of the questions | Have one or more following items during question and answer:<br>1) Not actively listen to questions<br>2) Do not provide knowledgeable answers<br>3) Unable to answer questions succinctly | Have one or more following items during question and answer:<br>1) Understands most questions<br>2) Responds somewhat knowledgeably<br>3) Answers questions somewhat succinctly | Listens to questions carefully and responds courteously, knowledgeably and clearly to questions. | Skillfully manages and responds to questions and also gauges questioners' understanding. | 1* |

*\*Note: In the original rubric PDF, the weightage for A3 is printed as 1, despite the criterion being worth 10 Marks. To yield 10 Marks, a weightage of 2 is mathematically required.*

| **B1** | **Clarity and fluency of Presentation (5 Marks)** | No presentation | Little evidences of clarity in project direction and planning.
