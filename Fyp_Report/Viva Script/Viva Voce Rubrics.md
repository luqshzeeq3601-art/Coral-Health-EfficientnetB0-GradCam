# FYP Viva Voce Rubrics

This document contains the complete content of the **Viva Voce Evaluation Rubrics** for the Final Year Project (FYP).

## Summary of Total Marks
- **Section A: Viva Q & A - Knowledge Depth:** 35 Marks (Weightage total: 6*)
- **Section B: Viva Oral Presentation:** 50 Marks (Weightage total: 10)
- **Total Marks:** **85 Marks** (Total Weightage: 16)
  
*\*Note: There is a discrepancy in Section A3 where the marks are allocated as 10 Marks, but the weightage in the rubric is listed as 1. A weightage of 2 is mathematically required to reach 10 Marks on a 0-5 scale.*

## C. Self-Assessment & Viva Preparation Map (Preparation Aid)

> **This section is a preparation aid, not part of the official rubric above.** The criteria in Sections A and B are reproduced verbatim from the official rubric and must not be changed. This map links the viva script (`FYP_Coral_Viva_Script_Guide_Deep_Questions_Spaced.docx`) and the 15-slide deck (`FYP-CORAL HEALTH AI VIVA.pptx`) to each criterion, and notes how to reach the top band (5 / Excellent).

| Item | Covered in viva pack | To reach the top band (5) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A1 — Technical knowledge (15)** | Every slide's *Expected Panel Questions*, especially the deep questions added to Slides 7, 8, 11 and 14. | Answer with **a number + a reason** (e.g., "98.11%, because 156 of 159 test images were correct"). Use the precise SWA / ensemble / TTA definitions in Slide 8 Q5. |

### Known weak points to rehearse

- **Small Dead class (150 images, 15 in the test set).** The 93.3% Dead recall rests on only 15 samples, so one error moves it ~6.7 points. Rehearse Slide 7 Q4 and Slide 11 Q5 (overfitting / reliability).
- **Most costly error type.** Two Bleached corals were predicted as Healthy — be ready to explain why that false-negative matters for conservation and how it is mitigated (Slide 11 Q4).
- **Reference count for B5.** Confirm the report meets the "15+ within 5 years" bar before the viva.
